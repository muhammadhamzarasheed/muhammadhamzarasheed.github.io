01The Eye

All games

Five rounds, one attempt each: a midpoint, a centre, three seconds, a circle, ten seconds. You estimate, the page measures, the gap is the score. The pointing rounds need a mouse, touch or stylus.

02Steady Hand

All games

Rest your pointer on the brass pad, then follow the wire to the ring without touching the edges of the channel. Three lives. Touches cost three seconds each. On a phone, trace with your finger and the channel is a little kinder. This one needs a mouse, touch or stylus.
