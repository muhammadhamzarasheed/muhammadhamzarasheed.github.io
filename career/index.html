Something for your career

Four tools · no sign up · your words stay on your device

The rest of this site argues that accuracy is a discipline. This page points the discipline at the job search: a CV built clean, a checklist that catches the usual failures, practice for the questions you already know are coming, and a ledger so the search itself reconciles. Everything runs here in your browser. What you type is never uploaded, sent or seen by anyone but you.
