Appendix G · a post-dated note

To the person you will be

Sealed in this browser · read by nobody · not even this site

Accountants call it a deferral: something real that you are not allowed to recognise yet. Write a note to your future self and choose the date it may be opened. It stays in this browser, unsent and unread. Clear your browser data and it is gone, so treat it like the private thing it is.

The seal is a promise you make to yourself. The page only keeps the date.
